# Homepage redesign

Mode: Persuade. Scope: one public homepage using the user-approved Editorial–Estate combination. Code-first; no new alternatives requested. Preserve free self-service registration, both audiences, and links to the interactive workspace preview.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A beautifully arranged event has a practical plan behind it. Let visitors move from the occasion to its working plan.

OWN-WORLD: Cream paper, deep forest-green fields, restrained rose accents, architectural photography, Libre Caslon display and readable Source Sans 3 controls. Shared brand and buttons connect the two surfaces.

STORY: Understand the event-planning offer immediately, inspect a clearly labelled working sample, then create an account.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Cream navigation; large left-aligned editorial headline with plain-language explanation and two actions; tall venue photograph to the right. A green planning strip bridges photography and product.

FORM: User-selected combination of existing Editorial (1) and Estate (2), explicitly confirmed as a single homepage plus workspace. No random seed: pinned existing direction, not a new visual-world competition. Signature interaction: switch occasion to change the sample plan and photography, with one restrained content transition.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
