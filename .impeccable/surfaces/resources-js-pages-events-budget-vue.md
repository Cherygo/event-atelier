# Event budget

Mode: Operate. Extend the established event workspace and budget preview with persisted planning. Code-first; preserve the user's approved Editorial–Estate system. No new identity or assets. Personal reference catalog has no saved entries.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A readable expense ledger separates estimates, actual costs, and recorded payments, without dressing bookkeeping as a promotional dashboard.

OWN-WORLD: Existing cream canvas, forest actions, sage summary field, rose attention labels, serif headings, sans-serif controls, thin ruled rows, tabular numerals.

STORY: Choose currency and target, list categorized expenses, refine actual costs, record payments, and see what remains unpaid. Unknown and zero remain distinct. No payment is sent.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Existing EventLayout and tabs; Budget heading and Add expense action; compact target/forecast information; ledger filters followed by named expenses and aligned amounts. Inline editors replace a row rather than open a modal. Mobile stacks labeled amounts and keeps every action in normal flow.

FORM: Ordinary extension of the user-selected Editorial–Estate workspace, no seed. Signature interaction: expanding an expense's payment record reveals its history in place; a successful record updates balances together. Reuse short workspace transitions with reduced-motion support.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
